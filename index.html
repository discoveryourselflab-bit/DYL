What is DYL

A structured, human-scale journey to find the intersection of your strengths, passions, and purpose.

Most of us move from school to college to work without ever asking the foundational questions: Who am I? What do I love? What kind of life do I want to build?

DYL is a held, supported space for exactly that inquiry — through self-discovery, apprenticeships, volunteering, and a community of people asking the same questions.

Who it's for

This programme is part of your discovery journey.

  • Young adults after Class 10 or 12 — unclear about college or direction
  • Graduates with a degree and no sense of what comes next
  • Mid-career professionals whose work no longer feels meaningful
  • Anyone ready to ask the real questions — no upper age limit

How you'll learn

Experience, reflection and community.

Experience, not lectures.

Hands-on exercises, reflection and conversation. You discover by doing.

Body and breath.

Simple Yoga techniques including breath and meditation that deepen week by week. A settled mind sees itself clearly.

Self-inquiry.

Guided exercises every week to map your strengths, energy and values.

A circle of seekers.

A mixed-age group where everyone learns from everyone.

Volunteering.

Pick a project alone or in a group. Guided reflections on what this brings up in you.

Daily practice.

Morning awareness capsule and an evening reflection in your DYL Diary.

Nature.

We meet in green, open spaces. Soil underfoot, sky overhead.

The format

12 Sundays in-person meeting over 3 months, in Bengaluru and outskirts.

Meet your facilitators

The people who will walk with you.

RV

Ravi Viswanathan

I've always been curious about what makes life meaningful and how we can make the most of the time and opportunities given to us.

My journey has taken me through many diverse worlds, from aerospace engineering to management consulting, corporate strategy, and eventually CSR and philanthropy. Looking back, each turn brought me a little closer to doing what I genuinely cared about, working with people and finding ways to make a difference.

Alongside this, Yoga has been a wonderful companion in my life. I'm a practitioner and teacher of classical Yoga in the Bihar/Satyananda tradition, which has helped me see Yoga as a way of living, learning and understanding myself, well beyond the mat.

Volunteering has been another great teacher. I recently wrote Anna: The Queen of Hearts – Embracing Life through Volunteering, a book inspired by an extraordinary life of service of my dear colleague and the joy that comes from giving ourselves to others.

These days, I find myself increasingly drawn to exploring the inner world, tending to my garden (both within and outside!), and discovering what it means to live with greater awareness, joy and purpose.

I'm looking forward to being part of DYL, sharing a little of what life has taught me, learning from everyone else's journeys, and exploring together a question I'm still discovering answers to: Who am I, and how can I bring more of myself into whatever I do?

AA

Aji Augustine

I've always been curious about people, what makes us who we are, what energises us, and where we naturally thrive.

Nearly two decades of working across CSR, social impact and the nonprofit world have brought me close to people from very different walks of life and deepened this curiosity. My own journey, along with my exploration of Neuro-Linguistic Programming, human behaviour, perception, communication and what shapes the way we think and make choices, has shaped how I understand and work with people today.

That journey eventually led me to co-found Bhumi & Akash and create DYL, a guided journey that helps people discover more about themselves, recognise what naturally energises them, and explore where they can truly thrive.

I don't see myself as someone with the answers. I see my role as listening deeply, asking thoughtful questions, offering different perspectives and helping you notice strengths and possibilities you may not have noticed before.

I'm not here to define your path. I'm here to walk alongside you as you discover more of it for yourself.

KS

Keerthana Sheshadri

My work has taken me across the corporate and social impact worlds over the last decade. Volunteering and my love for the outdoors have also been a big part of my life. Along the way, I've had the chance to meet people from different walks of life, each with their own ideas of what success and happiness look like.

Beyond work, purpose and all the things we set out to achieve, I have realised having fun is the most important part of living well. I love bringing people together, creating experiences and finding opportunities to learn through play.

This is what drew me to co-found Bhumi & Akash and create DYL. It brings together my love for building connections and creating shared experiences with the belief that discovering ourselves can be an exciting, playful and enriching experience. As a facilitator, I hope to create spaces where people feel comfortable being themselves, exploring new possibilities and connecting with others, while enjoying the process of discovering more about who they are.
